Understanding Phishing Attacks

Phishing is one of the most common cybersecurity threats. Learning how to recognise phishing attempts can help you protect your personal information and stay safe online.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack in which criminals pretend to be trusted organisations or individuals. They use fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, bank details and personal data.

How Do Phishing Attacks Work?

Attackers often send messages that appear to come from banks, delivery companies or popular online services. These messages may contain suspicious links or attachments. When a victim clicks a link, they may be taken to a fake website designed to steal their information.

Common Warning Signs

Be careful with unexpected messages, urgent requests, spelling mistakes, suspicious links and requests for passwords or verification codes. Always check the sender's email address before trusting a message.

How to Protect Yourself

Never click suspicious links or download unexpected attachments. Use strong and unique passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and verify unusual requests through official communication channels.
