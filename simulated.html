Simulated Phishing Exercise

Learn how to recognise suspicious emails by reviewing this fictional phishing email example.

Sample Suspicious Email

From: Hope Bridge Foundation - IT Support

To: Employee

Subject: Action Required: Account Security Review

Dear Team Member,

We are conducting a routine account security review to help protect our organisation's accounts.

Please review this notification and consider whether the message is legitimate before taking any action.

Urgent notice: Your account access may be interrupted if this request is ignored.

Regards,
IT Support Team

Training example only - no real account action is required.
