Learn how to identify phishing attempts and protect yourself from online threats.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of cyberattack in which criminals use fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

How to Stay Safe

Always check the sender's address, avoid suspicious links, never share passwords or verification codes, and verify unexpected requests before responding.
